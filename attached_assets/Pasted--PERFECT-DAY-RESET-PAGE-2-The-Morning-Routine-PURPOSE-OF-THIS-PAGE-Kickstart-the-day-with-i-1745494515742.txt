🕰️ 2. MORNING TIMELINE (7:00 – 10:30 AM)
Use visual time blocks (can be styled as a soft timeline or checklist):

✅ 7:00 – Wake Up
Use your alarm clock, not your phone.

Open the windows. Take 3 deep breaths.

Say: “Today, I reset.”

✅ 7:15 – Hydration Ritual
Drink a large glass of warm water with lemon & a pinch of sea salt

Optionally, add a drop of magnesium or electrolytes

Sit in silence for 5 minutes after drinking

✅ 7:30 – Light Movement
5–10 minutes of stretching or yoga

Focus on breath. Feel your body wake up.

Optional: follow our short YouTube video (if offline mode is skipped)

✅ 8:00 – Mind Reset
Write in a notebook (pen & paper — no phone)

3 things you're grateful for

1 intention for the day

1 affirmation (e.g., “I am clear, strong, and in control.”)

✅ 8:30 – Breakfast Time
(dynamic based on user's chosen diet type)

We provide 3 meal options – each balanced and energizing.
All are simple, affordable, and quick.

Omni Reset: Oatmeal with Greek yogurt, berries, walnuts, and honey

Vegetarian: Scrambled tofu with avocado & tomato on sourdough

Vegan: Smoothie bowl (banana, spinach, almond butter, chia, oat milk)

Gluten-Free: Omelette with mushrooms & herbs + fruit

Keto: Bulletproof coffee + scrambled eggs with greens

✅ 9:00 – Supplement Time
Take your core supplements with or after food:

Magnesium

B-Complex or B12

Ashwagandha

Zinc

Pau D’Arco tea (if possible)

✅ 9:30 – Walking Reset
20–30 minute walk — no podcast, no phone

Just walk. Breathe. Observe.

✅ 10:15 – Integration Moment
Sit quietly for 10 minutes. No agenda.

Let your body and brain settle.
