Design and implement a visually appealing, highly shareable digital "Certificate of Completion" for users who finish the 24-hour PerfectDayReset program.

📌 Key Goals:
- The certificate must be social media friendly.
- It should look premium, personal, and motivational.
- Encourage users to download and share it on platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.

🎨 Design Specifications:
- Size: Generate two formats:
  1. Square (1080x1080px) for social media feeds.
  2. Vertical (1080x1920px) for stories.
- Color scheme: Use gradient or glowing tones (e.g. sunrise orange, deep blue, soft gold) to symbolize transformation and energy.
- Fonts:
  - Name: Elegant script-style font (e.g., Playfair Display, Great Vibes).
  - Body text: Clean, modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Poppins).
- Background style: Subtle texture or soft abstract graphics (waves, energy lines, or sunrise).
- Include a modern digital **badge/seal** with text: “RESET: 24H Achiever”.

🧠 Dynamic Personalization (via user input):
- User's full name.
- Dynamic title earned (e.g. “Discipline Architect”, “Energy Alchemist”).
- A quote randomly selected from a list of motivational one-liners:
  - “In just 24 hours, I changed everything.”
  - “Reset. Rebuild. Rise.”
  - “I hit reset and found clarity.”

🖼️ Optional:
- Profile picture upload support: place user’s image in a clean circle frame on the top-right or bottom-left.

📲 Social Sharing Add-ons:
- A QR code linking to: `https://perfectdayreset.com`
- Small footer text: “You can do it too. Start your reset at perfectdayreset.com”
- A share button after generation with options to download in both sizes.

✨ Bonus Visual Flair:
- Optional subtle animation (pulse or glow effect).
- Hidden “easter egg” message layered subtly (e.g., “You’re more capable than you think”).

🛠️ Tools/Libraries Recommended:
- HTML/CSS/JS with Canvas or SVG.
- Use Google Fonts or embedded font files.
- Optional: integrate with a headless CMS or database to pull dynamic reflection-based titles or names.
