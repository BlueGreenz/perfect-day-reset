13:30
Cold Shower Reset
A 2-5 minute cold shower to boost circulation, mood, and immunity.

Benefits of Cold Exposure:
Increases alertness and energy
Reduces inflammation
Boosts immune function
Improves mood and mental clarity
Enhances circulation
Beginner's Guide
Start with a normal shower, then gradually turn temperature to cold for the last 30 seconds. Each day, increase cold exposure time until you can do 2-5 minutes. Focus on controlled breathing to manage the initial shock.
