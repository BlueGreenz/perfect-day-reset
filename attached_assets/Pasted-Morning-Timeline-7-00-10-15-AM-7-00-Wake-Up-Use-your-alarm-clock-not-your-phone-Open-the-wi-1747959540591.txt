Morning Timeline (7:00 – 10:15 AM)
7:00
Wake Up
Use your alarm clock, not your phone. Open the windows. Take 3 deep breaths. Say: "Today, I reset."

7:10
Hydration Ritual
Drink a large glass of warm water with lemon & a pinch of sea salt. Sit in silence for 5 minutes after drinking.

7:20
Light Movement
10 minutes of stretching or yoga. Focus on breath. Feel your body wake up. Optional: follow our short YouTube video (if offline mode is skipped): Morning Yoga Routine

7:30
Morning Grounding Walk
30-minute walk outside without your phone. Reconnect with nature and absorb morning light.
Why morning light exposure matters
Bright morning light advances your internal clock (earlier melatonin onset) and reduces "morning grogginess," leading to deeper, more restorative sleep at night.

Morning light exposure is linked to improved mood and lower risk of Seasonal Affective Disorder, as it increases serotonin production and stabilizes cortisol dynamics.

Grounding Tips & Benefits
Walk barefoot on grass, sand, or dip your feet in water if possible
Feel the earth beneath your feet and the air on your skin
Face the morning sun to help reset your circadian rhythm
Take deep breaths and notice the scents and sounds around you
Express gratitude for this moment of connection
Benefits of barefoot walking:

Connecting to the earth through barefoot contact allows electrons from the earth's surface to transfer to your body, potentially reducing inflammation and improving sleep quality.
